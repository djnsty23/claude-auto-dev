## Where the lesson goes

Pick the cheapest home that works. Always-on text is paid for on every turn of every session.

| The lesson is | Its home |
|---|---|
| A repeated mechanical step | A script or check that does it, and a pointer to it |
| A narrow procedure with a recognisable trigger | A skill section, named in that skill's triggers. It costs nothing until the situation arises |
| An invariant prose already states that sessions keep breaking (repeated evidence, not one slip) | A hook. It must not block valid work |
| Short and true in every session | One always-on line in `rules/`. Add one, cut or move one of similar size |
| A one-off slip | No entry |

Write the verified fact, labelled where it holds ("in vistek, doppler runs from its own
folder"), rather than the procedure for rediscovering it.
