## instructions-loaded loses rows when sessions load at the same moment

On ubuntu CI, twenty sessions loading an instruction file at once, against a
log already past the rotation size, kept only 11 of their 20 rows. The hook is
`plugins/autodev-core/hooks/instructions-loaded.js`. On Windows the same storm
has kept 20 of 20 every time, so it may not reproduce on your machine.

Looking at the logs directory after a bad run: besides the full segments there
was a small segment, far under the rotation size, holding the newest rows. Its
name sorted older than the full segment cut in the same burst. The next prune
deleted it, and those rows were gone.

Correct behaviour: a row a session just wrote is never lost to pruning. The
history on disk must still stay bounded. Full segments past the keep cap are
pruned as before, and a small segment is not kept forever either. Keep the
rotation size, the keep cap and the segment naming as they are.

## Interface the checks use

- A segment is "small" when it is under `ROTATE_BYTES`, and the cap on kept
  history is `KEEP_BYTES`. Both exist today.
- A small segment's age is its file modification time. The timestamp in its
  name is not a reliable age. One written moments ago must survive a prune
  even when it sorts past the cap. One last modified two hours ago or more must
  be pruned once it falls past the cap.
